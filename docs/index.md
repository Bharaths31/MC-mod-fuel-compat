# Immersive Aircraft Tech Compat

**Immersive Aircraft Tech Compat** is an addon designed to seamlessly bridge the gap between Immersive Aircraft and your favorite tech mods. 

Say goodbye to carrying stacks of coal! This mod allows you to power your gyrocopters, airships, and biplanes using the advanced liquid fuels produced by your tech infrastructure.

## Getting Started

### Installation
1. Install [Immersive Aircraft](https://modrinth.com/mod/immersive-aircraft).
2. Install [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
3. Drop the **Immersive Aircraft Tech Compat** `.jar` into your `mods` folder!

### Features
* **Extensive Mod Support:** Burn fuels from Tech Reborn, Oritech, and Modern Industrialization directly in your aircraft.
* **Reusable Fluid Containers:** Don't want to carry individual buckets? No problem! This mod natively hooks into Fabric's `FluidStorage` API, meaning you can place fluid containers directly into the aircraft's fuel slot. 
    * For example, **Tech Reborn Tank Units** are fully supported! The aircraft will intelligently sip fluid directly from the tank unit to fill its internal boiler, without consuming or deleting the tank item itself.
* **Highly Configurable:** Fine-tune the burn time multiplier of every single fuel type to perfectly balance your modpack.
